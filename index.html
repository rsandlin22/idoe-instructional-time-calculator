Instructional Time Recovery Calculator

Enter each school's grade span, current school-day length, and total days missed to see how many days IDOE will waive, how many must be made up, and how long recovery will take at the school's current schedule. Add a school for each building — elementary, middle, and high — to compare them side by side.

Waiver policy. The first 5 missed days must always be made up. Beyond that, IDOE waives every other missed day, up to 3 waived days total. Every remaining day beyond the 3 waivers must be made up.
Built from Indiana's statutory day-length minimums (5 hrs, grades 1–6; 6 hrs, grades 7–12) and IDOE's missed-day waiver policy as described. Verify against the current IDOE waiver guidance for the specific calendar event before submitting a corporation's recovery plan.
